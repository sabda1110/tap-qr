# Avatar toko oranye

Dibuat dengan built-in image_gen pada 26 Oktober 2026. Background transparan dipertahankan; output diperkecil ke WebP 256 × 256 untuk UI aktivasi. Tidak memakai avatar JPG lama.

- `new-store-orange.webp`: pilihan bisnis baru dan avatar di samping judul pemilihan bisnis.
- `existing-store-orange.webp`: pilihan bisnis terdaftar.

## Prompt bisnis baru

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: small square storefront avatar for TapQR business activation UI. Create one original welcoming small cafe storefront, front three-quarter view, burnt-orange and warm golden-orange striped awning, cream walls, warm wood door, tiny potted plant. Friendly hand-drawn 2D cartoon with bold clean near-black outlines, softly shaded flat warm colors matching an approachable small-business landing page. Dominant orange #ffb332 / #f28b35 and cream, no blue or cyan. Compact legible silhouette at 80px; building fills 85% of square. Isolated on genuinely transparent background. No lettering, no text, no signs with writing, no watermark, no people, no outer frame.

## Prompt bisnis terdaftar

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: second small square storefront avatar for TapQR existing-business selection. Create one original friendly established neighborhood shop storefront, front three-quarter view, orange terracotta pitched roof, golden-orange and cream striped awning, cream plaster walls, wood entrance and inviting display window, tiny plant beside door. Hand-drawn 2D cartoon, bold clean near-black outlines, soft flat warm shading, approachable Indonesian small-business brand. Match a warm orange landing-page illustration family: #ffb332 golden orange, #f28b35 orange, cream, warm brown, absolutely no blue or cyan. Building fills 85% of square, legible as a small 80px avatar. Isolated genuinely transparent background. No lettering, no readable text, no watermark, no people, no outer frame.
