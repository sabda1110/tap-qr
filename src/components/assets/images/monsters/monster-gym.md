# Monster Gym

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool for the TapQR Gym print template.
Project asset: `monster-gym.webp`, 512 × 512 pixels with transparency.
The original PNG is retained in the Codex generated_images directory.
Optimized as WebP quality 90, alpha quality 100; no background added.

## Generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: transparent mascot for TapQR printed
 gym review cards. Create a single friendly orange muscular gym monster lifting
 a black dumbbell in each hand, confident joyful grin with small cute teeth,
 two tiny horns, teal athletic headband and teal shorts. Full body, compact squat
 rounded proportions, thick near-black clean ink outlines, flat vector-like
 cartoon shapes with restrained cel shading, warm orange #ffb332 and #ff8a24,
 teal #0798ad, near-black #172029. Centered square composition, whole character
 and dumbbells visible, generous transparent padding, no ground or background,
 no text, no lettering, no logos, no QR, no watermark. Print-friendly crisp high
 contrast friendly fitness mascot, not frightening.

## Integration

`pvc-monster-gym` and `acrylic-monster-gym` use the shared GymMonsterCard renderer.
QR codes remain actual generated QR images on a white quiet zone; the mascot
contains no QR or embedded product copy. Text is localized in print-studio-id/en.
PVC stays 86 × 54 mm; acrylic uses the existing 380 × 550 artwork canvas and
physical print-size controls. The Studio mascot toggle is supported.

## Card composition

White surface and thick black comic framing match the original monster family.
The gym mascot sits in a circular badge above the headline and five decorative
stars. Weight plates decorate the corners; the portrait action ribbon sits
across barbell ends. NFC is a large white circular target beside a square white
QR frame. The landscape edition adapts this hierarchy to 86 × 54 mm.
