# Koleksi monster TapQR

Dibuat pada 26 Oktober 2026 dengan built-in `image_gen`, menggunakan latar
transparan. Hasil asli dipertahankan di direktori generated images Codex;
aset aplikasi dioptimalkan menjadi WebP 512 × 512 dengan alpha dipertahankan.
Teks, QR, bintang, bingkai, dan dekorasi kartu disusun dalam React/SVG,
sehingga QR dan isi cetak tetap dapat disesuaikan.

## Aset aplikasi

- `monster-mango.webp`: monster oranye bertanduk, dipakai pada tema Monster Mango.
- `monster-boba.webp`: monster teal dengan antena, dipakai pada tema Monster Boba.
- `monster-ziggy.webp`: monster kuning bermata satu, dipakai pada tema Monster Ziggy.

Ketiga maskot dipakai bersama oleh versi PVC, akrilik, dan thumbnail pemilih
tema. Jangan mengganti atau menimpa aset monster lama dengan aset koleksi ini.

## Prompt dasar untuk setiap aset

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: transparent mascot illustration for
TapQR NFC/QR print cards. Style: friendly quirky 2D cartoon sticker, very thick
clean dark #172029 ink outline, confident simple flat color fills, tiny minimal
highlights, graphic playful neo-brutalist print design. Single full-body
character centered, fills 85% square with breathing room, legible when printed
15mm tall. Genuine transparent alpha background. No text, no letters, no logos,
no QR code, no stars or floating objects outside character, no scenery, no
ground shadow, no border around canvas, no 3D or photorealism.

## Tambahan prompt Mango

Subject: one adorable orange mango-shaped monster with two little cream curved
horns, two oversized mismatched white eyes, a wide friendly toothy grin with a
couple of small teeth, tiny orange arms waving hello, short chunky feet, curled
tail. Body vivid orange #ff8a24, belly warm golden #ffb332. Expressive welcoming
silly face, soft rounded silhouette, all extremities fully inside canvas.

## Tambahan prompt Boba

Subject: one adorable teal boba-shaped round monster with two short rounded
antennae, two big white eyes, a broad friendly smiling mouth with two small
blunt teeth, small blush spots, chunky little paws and feet, one hand raised in
a welcoming wave, curled short tail. Body bright teal #27b9cf, mint-cream belly,
tiny warm golden #ffb332 accent on antenna tips. Compact stout silhouette,
approachable cute face, all extremities inside canvas.

## Tambahan prompt Ziggy

Subject: one adorable golden yellow playful monster with a zigzag crown-like
tuft of three soft rounded spikes, one big central white eye and expressive
eyebrow, friendly wide smile with three tiny blunt teeth, short arms posed
enthusiastically, chunky feet, small zigzag tail. Body golden yellow #ffb332,
orange #ff8a24 lower belly, tiny teal #27b9cf spots. Funny cheerful character,
no aggression. Compact readable fullbody silhouette, all extremities inside
canvas.
